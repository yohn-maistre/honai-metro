Natural Earth — 1:110m land polygons
Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Natural Earth raster and vector map data are in the public domain.

The small news place map uses rounded coordinates from the 1:110m land dataset,
clipped by the SVG viewport. No political borders or exact incident locations
are inferred. Story place coordinates come from WPW's published place metadata.
